Pişirsem Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Pişirsem is a mobile app that suggests meals, recipes and meal plans based on the ingredients you have at home. This policy explains what personal data the Pişirsem mobile app processes, why, on what legal basis, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what rights you have. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Data Controller

The controller of your personal data is Uğur Yeşilbaş (sole proprietorship, Türkiye), who develops and operates Pişirsem.
Contact for all privacy matters: support@pisirsem.com.tr

2. Website and App

The pisirsem.com.tr website is only used to present the app and publish legal texts. No user accounts are created on the website, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies. This policy mainly covers the Pişirsem mobile app.

We do not sell your personal data, do not show ads and do not use your data for advertising or profiling. Nutrition values (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat) are estimates and are not medical or dietary advice.

4. Service Providers and International Transfers

We use the following providers (processors) to run Pişirsem. They only receive the data needed for their service:

  • Google (Google Sign-In, Google Play billing): name, email, profile picture, purchase and subscription verification data.
  • OpenAI (AI suggestions in Pişirsem Plus and Athlete Mode): the ingredients, preferences and questions needed to create a suggestion, and your weight only if you entered it. Your name and email are never sent. Under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train their models.
  • Cloud hosting (application server and database): account, history, favourites, preferences, subscription and usage data.

Suggestions for guests and free users come from Pişirsem's own recipe collection; no data is sent to the AI service for them.

These providers may process data outside your country, mainly in the United States and the European Union. Where data from the EEA or the UK is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as offered by the provider. Transfers from Türkiye are made in line with Article 9 of the KVKK.

5. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account, history, favourites, preferences and Athlete Mode data: while your account exists. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Weight entries: until you delete them or your account.
  • Usage and security logs: only as long as needed for fair-use limits, security and preventing abuse.
  • Purchase and subscription records: as long as required by tax and commercial law.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws) you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for example for weight data), without affecting earlier processing;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, email support@pisirsem.com.tr from the address linked to your Pişirsem account. We answer within one month, free of charge. Users in Türkiye also have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

7. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app under Profile > Delete My Account, or see Account and Data Deletion. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days at the latest.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Children

Pişirsem is not directed at children under 13. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country (up to 16 in some EU countries), please use the app only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Automated Decisions

AI is used to create recipe and meal suggestions. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its date.
