Pan-Fried Lavash with Potato and Cheese

Pan-Fried Lavash with Potato and Cheese: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 35 min. With lavash, potato, kashar cheese, red pepper flakes. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 35 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings📂 Quick & Budget

Prep: 8 min · Cook: 27 min

Ingredients

  • lavash4 (about 280 g)
  • potato3 medium (about 540 g)
  • kashar cheese1 cup grated (about 100 g)
  • red pepper flakes1 tsp
  • butter1 tbsp
  • salt1 tsp (filling) + 2 tsp (boiling water)
  • water6 cups (for boiling)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Peel and dice the potatoes; bring 6 cups of water to the boil with 2 tsp salt, boil the potatoes for 15 minutes and mash.
  2. Mix 1 tsp salt and the pepper flakes into the potatoes.
  3. Spread the potato and kashar cheese over half of each lavash and fold in half.
  4. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the lavash for 3 minutes per side until golden; slice and serve.
