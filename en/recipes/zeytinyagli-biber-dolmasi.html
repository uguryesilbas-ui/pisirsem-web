Stuffed Peppers in Olive Oil

Stuffed Peppers in Olive Oil: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 1 hr 44 min. With bell pepper (for stuffing), rice, onion, tomato. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 1 hr 44 min👩‍🍳 Medium🍽️ 4 servings; 2-3 stuffed peppers per person📂 Vegetable Dishes

Prep: 20 min · Cook: 54 min · Rest: 30 min

Ingredients

  • bell pepper (for stuffing)10 (about 800 g)
  • rice1 cup
  • onion2 medium (about 300 g)
  • tomato1 medium (about 150 g)
  • pine nuts2 tbsp
  • currants1 tbsp
  • olive oil1/2 cup (half for the filling, half for cooking)
  • fresh mint2 tsp dried
  • parsley1/2 bunch (about 30 g)
  • allspice1/2 tsp
  • sugar1 tsp
  • salt2 tsp
  • water1/2 cup (filling) + 1.5 cups (cooking)
  • lemon1/2 (juiced)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds; keep the tops.
  2. Fry the onion in half of the olive oil for 6 minutes, add the pine nuts and stir for 2 minutes; add the rinsed rice and fry for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the currants, spices, sugar, salt and half a cup of water and cook for 8 minutes until the water is absorbed; fold in the parsley and mint.
  4. Fill the peppers three-quarters full with the filling, put the tops back on and stand them upright in a pot; place tomato slices on top.
  5. Add the remaining olive oil, 1.5 cups of hot water and the lemon juice, cover and cook over low heat for 35 minutes; serve once cooled.
