Date and Cocoa Energy Balls

Date and Cocoa Energy Balls: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 55 min. With dates, rolled oats, walnuts, cocoa powder. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 55 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings; about 16 balls📂 Desserts & Snacks

Prep: 15 min · Rest: 40 min

Ingredients

  • dates200 g (pitted, about 15)
  • rolled oats1 cup (about 90 g)
  • walnuts1/2 cup (about 60 g)
  • cocoa powder2 tbsp
  • desiccated coconut3 tbsp (for coating)
  • water2 cups (hot; only if the dates are hard)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. If the dates are hard, soak them in 2 cups of hot water for 10 minutes and drain.
  2. Blend the dates, oats, walnuts and cocoa in a food processor for 2 minutes until it forms a thick paste.
  3. Roll into 16 walnut-sized balls and coat them in the coconut.
  4. Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes and serve.
