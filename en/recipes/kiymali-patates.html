Potato and Ground Beef Stew

Potato and Ground Beef Stew: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 47 min. With ground beef, potato, onion, tomato paste. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 47 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Meat

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 37 min

Ingredients

  • ground beef250 g
  • potato5 medium (about 900 g)
  • onion1 medium (about 150 g)
  • tomato paste1 tbsp
  • sunflower oil2 tbsp
  • salt2 tsp
  • black pepper1/2 tsp
  • water1.5 cups

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Dice the potatoes and chop the onion.
  2. Fry the onion in the oil for 3 minutes, add the ground beef and cook for 8 minutes, then add the tomato paste and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add the potatoes, salt, black pepper and 1.5 cups of hot water.
  4. Cover and cook over low heat for 25 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
