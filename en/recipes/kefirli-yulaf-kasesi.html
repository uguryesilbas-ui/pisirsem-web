Kefir Oat Bowl

Kefir Oat Bowl: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 15 min. With kefir, rolled oats, banana, walnuts. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 15 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 bowls📂 Fitness Meals

Prep: 5 min · Rest: 10 min

Ingredients

  • kefir3 cups
  • rolled oats1 cup (about 90 g)
  • banana2 (about 240 g peeled)
  • walnuts1/3 cup (about 40 g)
  • cinnamon1/2 tsp
  • honey1 tbsp

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Mix the oats with the kefir and leave for 10 minutes to soften.
  2. Divide among 4 bowls.
  3. Add the sliced banana and crushed walnuts.
  4. Sprinkle with cinnamon, drizzle with honey and serve.
