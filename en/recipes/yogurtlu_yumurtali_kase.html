Boiled Eggs on Garlic Yogurt

Boiled Eggs on Garlic Yogurt: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 14 min. With eggs, yogurt, garlic, butter. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 14 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 bowls📂 Breakfast

Prep: 4 min · Cook: 10 min

Ingredients

  • eggs4
  • yogurt2 cups
  • garlic1 clove
  • butter1 tbsp
  • red pepper flakes1/2 tsp
  • dried mint1/2 tsp
  • salt1/4 tsp
  • water6 cups (for boiling)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Boil the eggs in 6 cups of boiling water for 9 minutes, transfer to cold water and peel.
  2. Whisk the yogurt with the crushed garlic and salt and divide among 4 bowls.
  3. Halve the eggs and place them on the yogurt.
  4. Sizzle the pepper flakes and mint in the butter for 30 seconds, drizzle over and serve.
