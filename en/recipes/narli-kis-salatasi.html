Winter Salad with Pomegranate

Winter Salad with Pomegranate: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 15 min. With lettuce, rocket (arugula), red cabbage, carrot. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 15 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ Side dish / salad for 4📂 Salads & Meze

Prep: 15 min

Ingredients

  • lettuce1 (about 400 g)
  • rocket (arugula)1 bunch (about 100 g)
  • red cabbage150 g
  • carrot1 medium (about 100 g)
  • pomegranate1 (about 300 g)
  • walnuts1/3 cup
  • pomegranate molasses2 tbsp
  • olive oil3 tbsp
  • salt1 tsp

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Wash the lettuce and rocket and chop roughly.
  2. Shred the red cabbage very finely and grate the carrot.
  3. Seed the pomegranate and break the walnuts into large pieces.
  4. Whisk the pomegranate molasses, olive oil and salt; toss everything with the dressing and serve right away.
