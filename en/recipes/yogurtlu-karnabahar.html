Cauliflower with Garlic Yogurt

Cauliflower with Garlic Yogurt: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 26 min. With cauliflower, yogurt, garlic, olive oil. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 26 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ Meze for 4📂 Salads & Meze

Prep: 8 min · Cook: 8 min · Rest: 10 min

Ingredients

  • cauliflower1/2 (about 600 g)
  • yogurt1.5 cups
  • garlic1 clove
  • olive oil2 tbsp
  • red pepper flakes1/2 tsp
  • salt1/2 tsp (yogurt) + 2 tsp (boiling water)
  • water8 cups (for boiling)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Break the cauliflower into small florets.
  2. Bring 8 cups of water to the boil with 2 tsp salt, boil the cauliflower for 6 minutes and drain; cool for 10 minutes.
  3. Whisk the yogurt with the crushed garlic and half a teaspoon of salt.
  4. Toss the cauliflower with the yogurt; sizzle the pepper flakes in the olive oil for 30 seconds and drizzle over.
