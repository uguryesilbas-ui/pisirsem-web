Lazy Mantı (Pasta with Ground Beef and Garlic Yogurt)

Lazy Mantı (Pasta with Ground Beef and Garlic Yogurt): an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 25 min. With fusilli, ground beef, onion, olive oil. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 25 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Pasta & Rice

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 20 min

Ingredients

  • fusilli400 g
  • ground beef250 g
  • onion1 small (about 100 g)
  • olive oil1 tbsp (for the mince)
  • yogurt2 cups
  • garlic2 cloves
  • butter2 tbsp (sauce)
  • tomato paste2 tsp
  • red pepper flakes1 tsp
  • dried mint1 tsp
  • salt1/2 tsp (mince) + 2 tsp (pasta water)
  • water10 cups (for boiling the pasta)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Bring 10 cups of water to the boil with 2 tsp salt, cook the pasta for 9 minutes (as per the packet) and drain.
  2. While the pasta cooks, stir the grated onion in 1 tbsp olive oil for 3 minutes, add the ground beef and fry for 8 minutes until the liquid evaporates; season with half a teaspoon of salt.
  3. Whisk the yogurt with the crushed garlic.
  4. Sizzle the tomato paste, pepper flakes and dried mint in the butter for 1 minute.
  5. Divide the pasta among plates and top with the beef, yogurt and sauce, in that order, and serve.
