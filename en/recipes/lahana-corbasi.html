Black Sea Cabbage Soup

Black Sea Cabbage Soup: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 1 hr 10 min (plus overnight soaking). With black cabbage (kale), dried borlotti beans, cornmeal, onion. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 1 hr 10 min💧 Soak overnight (at least 8 hours), for the beans👩‍🍳 Medium🍽️ 4 bowls of soup📂 Soups

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 1 hr

Ingredients

  • black cabbage (kale)1 bunch (about 500 g)
  • dried borlotti beans1/2 cup
  • cornmeal2 tbsp
  • onion1 medium (about 150 g)
  • tomato paste1 tbsp
  • butter2 tbsp
  • red pepper flakes1 tsp
  • salt2 tsp
  • water7 cups (soup) + 4 cups (for boiling the beans) + 1/2 cup (cold, for the cornmeal)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Drain the borlotti beans soaked overnight and boil them with 4 cups of water in a pressure cooker for 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Remove the thick stems of the black cabbage, finely chop the leaves and wash them.
  3. Fry the onion in the butter for 4 minutes, add the tomato paste and pepper flakes and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add the cabbage, the cooked beans and 7 cups of water and cook for 25 minutes.
  5. Mix the cornmeal with 1/2 cup of cold water and stir it in; season with salt and cook for 10 more minutes.
