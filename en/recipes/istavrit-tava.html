Pan-Fried Horse Mackerel

Pan-Fried Horse Mackerel: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 33 min. With horse mackerel, cornmeal, salt, frying oil. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 33 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Fish & Seafood

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 18 min

Ingredients

  • horse mackerel1 kg (cleaned)
  • cornmeal1 cup
  • salt2 tsp
  • frying oil1.5 cups (for frying; about 4 tbsp ends up in the dish)
  • lemon1 (to serve)
  • red onion1 (about 150 g)
  • parsley1/2 bunch (about 30 g)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Wash the cleaned horse mackerel and leave in a colander for 10 minutes to drain.
  2. Toss the fish with the salt and coat well in the cornmeal.
  3. Heat the frying oil in a wide pan and fry the fish in three batches, 3 minutes per side.
  4. Drain on kitchen paper and serve hot with finely sliced onion, parsley and lemon.
