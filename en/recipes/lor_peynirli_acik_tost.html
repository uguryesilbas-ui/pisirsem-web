Open Toast with Lor Cheese

Open Toast with Lor Cheese: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 13 min. With wholemeal bread, lor cheese (ricotta-style), tomato, olive oil. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 13 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 slices (1 per person)📂 Breakfast

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 8 min

Ingredients

  • wholemeal bread4 slices (about 160 g)
  • lor cheese (ricotta-style)200 g
  • tomato1 medium (about 150 g)
  • olive oil1 tbsp
  • thyme1/2 tsp
  • salt1/4 tsp

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees; slice the tomato into thin rounds.
  2. Mix the lor cheese with the olive oil and salt and spread it thickly on the bread.
  3. Arrange the tomatoes on top and sprinkle with thyme.
  4. Bake on a tray for 8 minutes until the edges are golden, and serve hot.
