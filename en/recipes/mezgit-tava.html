Pan-Fried Whiting

Pan-Fried Whiting: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 31 min. With whiting, flour, salt, frying oil. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 31 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Fish & Seafood

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 21 min

Ingredients

  • whiting1 kg (cleaned)
  • flour1 cup
  • salt2 tsp
  • frying oil1.5 cups (for frying; about 4 tbsp ends up in the dish)
  • lemon1 (to serve)
  • rocket (arugula)1 bunch, about 100 g (to serve)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Wash the whiting, pat completely dry with kitchen paper and season with salt.
  2. Coat the fish in the flour and shake off the excess.
  3. Heat the frying oil in a pan and fry the fish in three batches, 3-4 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Transfer to kitchen paper and serve hot with rocket and lemon.
