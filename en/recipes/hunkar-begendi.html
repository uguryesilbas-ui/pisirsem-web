Hünkar Beğendi (Beef Stew on Smoky Eggplant Purée)

Hünkar Beğendi (Beef Stew on Smoky Eggplant Purée): an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 1 hr 30 min. With diced beef, onion, tomato, tomato paste. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 1 hr 30 min👩‍🍳 Hard🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Meat

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 1 hr 15 min

Ingredients

  • diced beef500 g (lamb or beef)
  • onion1 medium (about 150 g)
  • tomato2 medium (about 300 g)
  • tomato paste1 tbsp
  • eggplant4 large, for roasting (about 1.2 kg)
  • milk2 cups
  • flour2 tbsp
  • butter3 tbsp (1 for the meat, 2 for the eggplant purée)
  • kashar cheese1/2 cup grated (about 50 g)
  • salt2 tsp (half for the meat, half for the eggplant purée)
  • water2 cups

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Fry the meat in 1 tbsp butter for 10 minutes; add the chopped onion, tomato paste, grated tomato and half the salt and cook for 5 minutes, then add 2 cups of water and cook over low heat for 60 minutes.
  2. While the meat cooks, char the eggplants over a gas flame or in a 220-degree oven for 25 minutes until the skins blacken; peel and chop with a knife.
  3. Cook the flour in 2 tbsp butter for 2 minutes, then whisk in the milk little by little; once thickened, add the eggplant.
  4. Cook the purée for 5 minutes, then fold in the kashar cheese and the remaining salt and stir until the cheese melts.
  5. Spread the purée on a plate, place the meat in the centre and serve hot.
