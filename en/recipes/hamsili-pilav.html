Hamsili Pilav (Anchovy-Wrapped Rice)

Hamsili Pilav (Anchovy-Wrapped Rice): an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 1 hr 30 min. With anchovies, rice, onion, butter. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 1 hr 30 min👩‍🍳 Hard🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Fish & Seafood

Prep: 30 min · Cook: 50 min · Rest: 10 min

Ingredients

  • anchovies750 g (cleaned, boneless)
  • rice2 cups
  • onion1 medium (about 150 g)
  • butter3 tbsp (2 for the pilaf, 1 for the mould)
  • pine nuts2 tbsp
  • currants1 tbsp
  • allspice1 tsp
  • black pepper1/2 tsp
  • salt2 tsp (pilaf) + 2 tsp (rice soaking water)
  • water3 cups (pilaf) + 4 cups (for soaking the rice, lukewarm)
  • dill1/2 bunch (about 25 g)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Soak the rice in 4 cups of lukewarm water with 2 tsp salt for 20 minutes and drain; preheat the oven to 190 degrees.
  2. Fry the onion and pine nuts in 2 tbsp butter for 5 minutes, add the rice and stir for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the currants, spices, 2 tsp salt and 3 cups of hot water and cook over low heat for 12 minutes until the water is absorbed; fold in the chopped dill.
  4. Grease a mould with 1 tbsp butter; line the bottom and sides with anchovies, skin side out, fill with the rice and cover the top with anchovies.
  5. Bake at 190 degrees for 30 minutes, rest for 10 minutes and turn out onto a serving plate.
