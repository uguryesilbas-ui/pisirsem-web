Baked Eggplant and Tomatoes

Baked Eggplant and Tomatoes: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 55 min. With eggplant, tomato, onion, garlic. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 55 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main or side dish)📂 Vegetable Dishes

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 40 min

Ingredients

  • eggplant3 medium (about 900 g)
  • tomato3 medium (about 450 g)
  • onion1 medium (about 150 g)
  • garlic2 cloves
  • olive oil4 tbsp
  • salt2 tsp
  • thyme1 tsp
  • kashar cheese1/2 cup grated (about 50 g)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees; slice the eggplants and tomatoes into 1 cm rounds and the onion into thin rings.
  2. Stand the eggplant, tomato and onion slices upright in a baking dish, alternating them.
  3. Mix the olive oil, crushed garlic, salt and thyme and drizzle over the vegetables.
  4. Bake at 200 degrees for 35 minutes; sprinkle with the kashar cheese and bake for 5 more minutes.
