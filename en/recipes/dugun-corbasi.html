Düğün Çorbası (Wedding Soup)

Düğün Çorbası (Wedding Soup): an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 1 hr 10 min. With bone-in beef, flour, yogurt, eggs. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 1 hr 10 min👩‍🍳 Medium🍽️ 4 bowls of soup📂 Soups

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 55 min

Ingredients

  • bone-in beef400 g (lamb)
  • flour2 tbsp
  • yogurt1 cup
  • eggs1 (yolk only)
  • lemon1 (juiced)
  • butter2 tbsp
  • paprika1 tsp
  • salt2 tsp
  • water8 cups (for boiling the meat) + 1 cup (cold, for the egg-lemon sauce)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Boil the meat with 8 cups of water in a pressure cooker for 40 minutes; once the steam is released, lift out the meat and shred it, and strain the stock.
  2. Whisk the flour, yogurt, egg yolk and lemon juice with 1 cup of cold water until smooth.
  3. Temper the mixture with 2 ladles of the hot stock and add it to the pot while stirring constantly.
  4. Add the shredded meat and salt and simmer over low heat for 10 minutes.
  5. Sizzle the paprika in the butter for 30 seconds, drizzle over the soup and serve.
