Avocado Toast with Eggs

Avocado Toast with Eggs: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 14 min. With wholemeal bread, avocado, eggs, lemon. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 14 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 slices (1 per person)📂 Breakfast

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 9 min

Ingredients

  • wholemeal bread4 slices (about 160 g)
  • avocado2 (about 300 g)
  • eggs4
  • lemon1/2 (juiced)
  • salt1/2 tsp
  • black pepper1/4 tsp
  • red pepper flakes1/4 tsp
  • water6 cups (for boiling)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Boil the eggs in 6 cups of boiling water for 6 minutes for soft-boiled, then transfer to cold water.
  2. Toast the bread in a toaster or a pan for 3 minutes.
  3. Mash the avocado with a fork with the lemon juice and salt, and spread it on the toast.
  4. Peel the eggs, halve them and place them on the toast; serve sprinkled with black pepper and pepper flakes.
