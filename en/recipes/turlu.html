Türlü (Mixed Vegetable Stew)

Türlü (Mixed Vegetable Stew): an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 1 hr 15 min. With eggplant, zucchini, potato, green beans. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 1 hr 15 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main dish)📂 Vegetable Dishes

Prep: 20 min · Cook: 45 min · Rest: 10 min

Ingredients

  • eggplant1 medium (about 300 g)
  • zucchini2 medium (about 500 g)
  • potato2 medium (about 360 g)
  • green beans200 g
  • pepper3 long green peppers (about 60 g)
  • onion1 medium (about 150 g)
  • tomato3 medium (about 450 g)
  • tomato paste1 tbsp
  • olive oil1/3 cup
  • salt2 tsp (for the dish) + 1 tbsp (eggplant soaking water)
  • water1 cup (cooking) + 5 cups (for soaking the eggplant)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Peel the eggplant in stripes and dice it; soak it in 5 cups of water with 1 tbsp salt for 15 minutes, then squeeze it out.
  2. Fry the onion in the olive oil for 4 minutes, add the tomato paste and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add the chopped green beans and potatoes and stir for 5 minutes; then add the eggplant, zucchini and peppers.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, 2 tsp salt and 1 cup of water, cover and cook over low heat for 35 minutes.
  5. Take off the heat, rest for 10 minutes and serve with yogurt or rice.
