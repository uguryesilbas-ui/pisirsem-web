Chickpea and Chicken Salad

Chickpea and Chicken Salad: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 40 min. With cooked chickpeas, chicken breast, lettuce, tomato. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 40 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ 4 servings (main-course salad)📂 Pulses

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 20 min · Rest: 10 min

Ingredients

  • cooked chickpeas1.5 cups (canned or pre-cooked)
  • chicken breast400 g
  • lettuce1 (about 400 g)
  • tomato2 medium (about 300 g)
  • cucumber2 (about 300 g)
  • lemon1 (juiced)
  • olive oil4 tbsp
  • salt1 tsp
  • water5 cups (for poaching the chicken)

1 cup = 200 ml (a Turkish water glass). tbsp = tablespoon, tsp = teaspoon.

Instructions

  1. Boil the chicken in 5 cups of boiling water for 20 minutes; let it cool for 10 minutes and shred it.
  2. Roughly chop the lettuce and dice the tomato and cucumber.
  3. Combine the chickpeas, vegetables and shredded chicken in a large bowl.
  4. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil and salt, pour over the salad and serve right away.
