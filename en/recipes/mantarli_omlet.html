Mushroom Omelette

Mushroom Omelette: an easy Turkish home-cooking recipe ready in 15 min. With eggs, mushrooms, milk, olive oil. Simple step-by-step instructions, serves 4.

⏱️ 15 min👩‍🍳 Easy🍽️ Breakfast for 4📂 Breakfast

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 10 min

Ingredients

  • eggs6
  • mushrooms250 g
  • milk3 tbsp
  • olive oil2 tbsp
  • salt1 tsp (half for the eggs, half for the mushrooms)
  • black pepper1/2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Thinly slice the mushrooms; whisk the eggs with the milk, half the salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan and sauté the mushrooms for 6 minutes until they release and lose their liquid; sprinkle with the remaining salt.
  3. Pour the eggs over the mushrooms.
  4. Cover and cook over low heat for 4 minutes; fold, slice and serve.
