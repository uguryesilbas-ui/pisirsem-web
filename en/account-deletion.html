Pişirsem Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 28 October 2026

No user accounts are created on the pisirsem.com.tr website. This page explains how users who signed in to the Pişirsem mobile app with Google can delete their account and the data linked to it.

How to Request Deletion

In the app: Profile > Delete My Account.

By email: support@pisirsem.com.tr. Please write from, or include, the email address you use for your Pişirsem account so we can verify the account.

What Is Deleted

Your profile, recipe history, favourites, saved recipes, Athlete Mode history and profile, weight entries, Plus preferences and family profile, weekly menus, the ingredients you entered and your app preferences.

What May Be Kept

Some records may be kept for a limited time where required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or payment and subscription records. They are kept only for these purposes.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in Google Play (Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions).

Timing

Deletion requests are completed within 30 days at the latest. We may ask for more information to confirm you own the account.

Contact

Email: support@pisirsem.com.tr
